Artify is considering material durability, public access and your bronze practice.

The proposed sculpture will be cast in bronze and designed for permanent outdoor installation along the South Harbour promenade. Its scale will invite movement around the work, allowing the changing light across the water to become part of the piece.

The form will be developed through Anna Mikelsons' ongoing research into memory held in material. A concealed stainless-steel armature and sealed patina will support longevity in Helsinki's coastal climate, with a low-maintenance surface treatment specified for the first five years.
